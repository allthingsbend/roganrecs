## Optimizing Health and Brain Function: A Look at Dr. Andrew Huberman's Supplement Recommendations

_Updated 5/9/24_

Dr. Andrew Huberman, a celebrated neuroscientist and professor at Stanford University, has emerged as a guiding light in the realm of neuroscience and health optimization. His insights into the role of supplements in enhancing brain function and overall health have garnered significant attention. This blog delves into his supplement recommendations, offering a window into his scientifically-backed approach to health and wellness.

If you watch Huberman videos, chances are you’ve tried some new supplements to add to the daily routine!

### Key Supplements Recommended by Dr. Huberman

#### Focus & Cognition

-   Alpha-GPC: A go-to for intense workout or mental work sessions, Alpha-GPC enhances cognitive function and motivation.

-   L-Tyrosine: Used occasionally for boosting focus and attention, it's a precursor to dopamine.

-   Phenylethylamine (PEA): Increases dopamine, improving mood and attention, especially when combined with Alpha-GPC​​.


#### Hormone Support

-   Tongkat Ali: Believed to increase testosterone levels and improve sexual performance.

-   Fadogia Agrestis and Zinc: Both supplements aid in maintaining hormone balance and overall health​​.


#### General Health and Wellness

-   Omega-3 Fatty Acids, Vitamin D, and Magnesium: These are essential for cognitive performance, overall health, and reducing anxiety​​.


#### Digestion and Gut Health

-   AG1 (Athletic Greens): Replaces traditional probiotic supplements and supports gut microbiome.

-   Multivitamins and Protein Powder: Huberman incorporates these into his routine for additional nutrient support​​.


#### Hydration and Electrolyte Balance

-   Electrolyte Powder (LMNT Electrolyte Drink Mix): Maintains cognitive function and reduces stress and anxiety.

-   Essential Amino Acids (EAAs): Used post-workout for rehydration and performance​​.


### Scientific Basis and Research

Huberman's recommendations are rooted in modern neuroscience and human physiology. He emphasizes supplements that have proven benefits, such as Omega-3, Vitamin D3, Magnesium, and unique choices like Uridine and Apigenin. His focus extends to cognitive health with recommendations like Bacopa Monnieri and Lion's Mane, alongside energy-enhancing supplements like Coenzyme Q10 and PQQ​​.

It's crucial to remember that individual needs vary greatly. Huberman's regime acts more as a guideline rather than a one-size-fits-all solution. Before starting any new supplement regimen, consulting with a healthcare provider is strongly recommended to ensure safety and appropriateness based on individual health conditions and needs.
