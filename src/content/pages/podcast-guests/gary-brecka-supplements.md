Gary Brecka has been on the Joe Rogan Experience twice, and the first visit in particular was packed with product recommendations: hydrogen water bottles, methylated vitamins, red light panels and a specific sleep stack.

This page collects what he actually recommended on air, in one place, and puts an honest evidence label next to each item. Some of his advice is solid and cheap. Some is expensive and thinly supported. It helps to know which is which before you spend.

> **The short version:** On [JRE #2060](https://podcastnotes.org/joe-rogan-experience/2060-gary-brecka-joe-rogan-experience/) Brecka's protocol was **methylated vitamins, a short sleep stack, electrolytes, cold, red light, morning sun, grounding and hydrogen water**. The free parts (sunlight, cold showers, breathwork) and fixing real deficiencies are the best-supported. Hydrogen water and grounding have the weakest evidence.

## The supplements

### A methylated multivitamin

Brecka's core idea is methylation: some people process certain B vitamins less efficiently, so he prefers "active" forms like methylfolate and methylcobalamin. He named **Thorne** and **Pure Encapsulations**.

A good multivitamin is reasonable insurance. The "methylated" part matters most for people with a real, diagnosed problem processing folate, which is far less common than the internet suggests. Thorne's [Basic Nutrients 2/Day](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00FOTMGTU) is a common pick that uses active forms.

[Check Thorne Basic Nutrients 2/Day on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00FOTMGTU)

### Methylfolate instead of folic acid

He told listeners to remove synthetic folic acid and use methylated folate, around 800 mcg a day depending on body weight. This is where we'd disagree. Folic acid fortification has a long track record of preventing birth defects, and most people, including most with common MTHFR variants, use it fine. If you're pregnant or planning to be, follow your doctor, not a podcast. Our [methylated vitamins guide](/blog/methylated-vitamins) goes deeper.

### The sleep stack: magnesium threonate, SAM-e, zinc

- **Magnesium L-threonate** is a form marketed for brain and sleep. Magnesium in general is reasonable to try if your diet is low in it. [Magtein-based threonate](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B336FCWC) is the common version.
- **SAM-e** is the surprising one here. It has been studied mainly for mood, joints and liver, not sleep, and some people find it stimulating. It also interacts with antidepressants. Read our [SAM-e guide](/blog/sam-e-supplement-gary-brecka) before taking it.
- **Zinc** is worth it if you're low, and not much otherwise. Long-term high doses can cause copper deficiency.

### Electrolytes

He named **LMNT**, **10X** and plain **Celtic salt**. For people who sweat heavily, train hard or eat low-carb, sodium and potassium make a noticeable difference. This is one of his easiest recommendations to justify. Rogan's own routine includes electrolytes too, as covered in our [supplements guide](/supplements).

[Check the LMNT variety pack on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084HQ4DYQ)

## The water advice

Brecka's water ranking on #2060: **hydrogen water first, then spring water, then alkaline, and avoid tap water**, which he tied to fluoride. He named the **Echo** hydrogen bottle and **Mountain Valley** spring water, and the second episode spent a full segment on molecular hydrogen.

Hydrogen water is the most-searched thing to come out of his appearances, and the evidence is the thinnest: small studies, inconsistent results, nothing that supports the big claims. Our [hydrogen water guide](/blog/hydrogen-water-gary-brecka) covers what the research does and doesn't show. If you want to try it anyway, the [Echo Go+](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CW9HJTP5) is the bottle he's associated with.

## The free stuff is the best stuff

The most defensible part of Brecka's protocol costs nothing:

- **Morning sunlight** helps set your body clock and is well-supported for sleep.
- **Breathwork** and slow nasal breathing can calm you down. See our page on [mouth taping and James Nestor](/joe-rogan-mouth-tape) for the bigger picture.
- **Cold showers** are a free way to try cold exposure before buying a plunge.
- **Grounding**, standing barefoot on earth, is harmless and pleasant. The health claims behind it are weak.

## Where we'd push back

- **Genetic tests as a starting point.** For most healthy people, standard bloodwork tells you more for less money than a methylation panel.
- **SAM-e for sleep.** It's not where the evidence for SAM-e is, and the drug interactions are real.
- **Tap water fear.** Municipal water in most of the US is safe. A filter is a reasonable upgrade; fear isn't.
- **Hydrogen water as a health priority.** If you're spending on water before sleep, protein and training, the order is backward.

None of this means Brecka is wrong about everything. His emphasis on sleep, sunlight, movement and cutting ultra-processed food is mainstream advice in different packaging. The problems start when inexpensive basics get bundled with expensive gadgets as if they were equally proven.
