The chair most closely associated with Joe Rogan's JRE studio is the **HÅG Capisco**, the distinctive saddle-seat task chair from Norwegian brand HÅG/Flokk.

It looks unusual because it is not designed like a normal executive chair. The Capisco is built around **changing positions**: sit forward, turn sideways, perch higher, or sit backward using the backrest as chest support.

> **Quick take:** The Capisco is a legitimately interesting chair, but it is not automatically the “best office chair” because Rogan uses it. Buy it if you like active sitting and a sit-stand setup. Try before buying if you can.

## Which HÅG Capisco does Joe Rogan use?

The classic model most commonly associated with the JRE setup is the **HÅG Capisco 8106**, though upholstery, lift height, base and studio furniture can change over time.

That distinction matters less than shoppers think. If you are buying one for yourself, focus on:

- the **seat-height range**
- standard vs. tall gas lift
- whether you use a sit-stand desk
- upholstery and seat feel
- footring compatibility
- return policy

The exact color seen in a podcast clip is not what determines whether the chair fits you.

## Why the Capisco looks like a saddle

Designer Peter Opsvik drew inspiration from a horseback-riding posture. Instead of a broad flat seat, the Capisco uses a shaped saddle seat and a T-style backrest.

The design makes it easy to:

- sit conventionally
- sit higher with a more open hip angle
- turn sideways
- sit backward against the backrest
- move between sitting and perching

That is the real ergonomic idea: **variation instead of one supposedly perfect posture**.

## Is the HÅG Capisco actually ergonomic?

“Ergonomic” does not mean universally comfortable.

The Capisco can work well for people who:

- dislike sinking into a deep office chair
- use a sit-stand desk
- prefer frequent position changes
- like firmer, more active seating
- want to sit higher than a standard task-chair range

It may be a bad fit if you want:

- a plush seat
- a headrest
- large padded armrests
- deep recline
- a broad conventional seat pan

The most important ergonomic feature of any chair is whether **you can sit in it comfortably and keep moving throughout the day**.

## The biggest buying mistake: choosing the wrong lift height

A Capisco can feel terrible if the height setup does not match your desk.

Before ordering, measure:

1. your desk height
2. the seat-height range you normally use
3. whether your feet will rest on the floor or need a footring
4. whether you plan to use it at standing-desk height

The taller lift can be great for perching at an elevated desk but awkward if your workstation is set up like a standard seated desk.

## Is the HÅG Capisco worth it?

### It is probably worth considering if:

- you already know you like saddle seating
- you have a sit-stand desk
- you change position constantly
- you care about long-term furniture quality
- the design itself matters to you

### It is probably not worth it if:

- you mainly want “Joe Rogan's chair” as a novelty
- you want plush comfort
- you are buying blind with no return option
- a normal ergonomic chair already works well for you

## How to set it up

Once you have one:

- adjust the height so your feet are supported
- keep the desk high enough that your shoulders stay relaxed
- use the saddle shape without forcing your hips too wide
- rotate positions throughout the day
- stand and walk regularly

No chair eliminates the need to move.
