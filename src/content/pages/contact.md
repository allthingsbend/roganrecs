Email is the fastest way to reach us: **hello@roganrecs.com**

## Corrections come first

If something on this site is wrong, out of date, or attributes a recommendation
to the wrong person, that's the message we most want to receive. A link to the
page and a note on what's off is plenty — you don't need to make a case for it.

Corrections get made and the "Updated" date on the page changes so readers can
see it's been touched.

## Suggestions

If something came up on a recent episode and there's no page for it here, say so.
Episode number or a rough timestamp helps a lot, but isn't required.

## Brands and partnerships

We do work with affiliate programs, and that's disclosed at the top of every page
carrying an affiliate link.

What we don't do is sponsored posts that read like editorial, paid placement in a
"best of" list, or link insertions into existing content. If that's the enquiry,
this will be a short conversation.

## What we can't help with

We have no connection to Joe Rogan, the Joe Rogan Experience, Spotify, the
Comedy Mothership, or the UFC. We can't pass on messages, get you booked, sort
out ticket problems, or answer anything about the show itself. This is an
independent fan site and the people behind it have exactly as much access as you
do.
