Most celebrity cardio endorsements read like ad copy. Rogan's is one sentence, and it's the best description of an air bike anyone has written.

In 2020 he posted that the Rogue Echo bike is "a superb mood enhancement device disguised as a cardio machine."

Anyone who has done intervals on one knows what he means. Thirty seconds on an air bike is miserable. Twenty minutes later you feel great. The Echo sits in his studio gym next to the rowers and runners, and it's the one he chose to talk up.

> **The short version:** Rogan's bike is the [Rogue Echo](https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-echo-bike), sold by Rogue directly rather than on Amazon. The closest Amazon alternatives are the [Assault AirBike Classic](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00F74RX40) and the [Schwinn Airdyne AD7](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000E158CK). Start with short intervals and keep most rides easy.

## What makes an air bike different

An air bike has a big fan where a flywheel would be, and moving handles you push and pull while you pedal. That setup does two things:

- **The resistance is you.** The harder you go, the more air the fan pushes, the harder it gets. There's no dial to hide behind.
- **It's full body.** Arms and legs both work, so your heart rate climbs faster than on a normal bike.

That's why it works for someone like Rogan, who trains martial arts, lifts and wants conditioning that doesn't pound his knees the way running does.

## How to train on it without hating it

Air bikes punish ego. The most common mistake is going all-out for 15 seconds and then crawling for the rest of the workout. Start slower than feels necessary.

**Intervals (once or twice a week)**
- 10 rounds: 20 seconds hard, 40 seconds easy
- Build to 15 rounds over a few weeks before making the hard parts harder

**Easy rides (two or three times a week)**
- 20 to 40 minutes at a pace where you could hold a conversation
- This is where most of the fitness comes from, and it's the "mood enhancement" part without the suffering

**The test that humbles everyone**
- 10 minutes for max calories, once a month. Write it down. Try to beat it.

Set the seat so your knee keeps a slight bend at the bottom of the pedal stroke, like a road bike.

## Also in his gym: the AirRunner

Rogan's cardio corner includes an [Assault AirRunner](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B075MZ6TF3), a curved, motorless treadmill you power yourself. It's the running version of the same idea: you set the pace, and it gets hard fast. It's a bigger purchase and takes more floor space, so most people should start with a bike.
