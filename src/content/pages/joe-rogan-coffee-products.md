## Best overall starting point: Black Rifle Coffee

If the reason you landed here is simply that you want to try a coffee with a clear Rogan/JRE connection, **Black Rifle Coffee** is the cleanest place to start. Rogan directly identified Black Rifle as coffee being used in the studio in JRE #2170.

We would not claim that one roast is his permanent favorite. For a normal shopper, the better question is which roast fits your taste and brewing method.
