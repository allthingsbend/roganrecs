Rogan Recs started because the same questions kept going unanswered. Someone
would mention a supplement, a sauna protocol, a pair of boots, and by the time
you wanted to look it up you'd need to scrub through three hours of audio to find
the twenty seconds where it was actually discussed.

So this is the index nobody else was keeping.

## What this site is not

It is not affiliated with Joe Rogan, the Joe Rogan Experience, Spotify, Onnit, or
any brand covered here. Nobody at Rogan Recs has met or spoken to him. This is a
fan project, written by someone who listens to the show.

It is also not a medical resource. A meaningful share of what gets discussed on
the podcast sits in territory where the evidence is thin, early, or contested —
peptides, stem cells, hydrogen water, various nootropics. The guides here try to
be straight about that distinction rather than laundering podcast enthusiasm into
something that reads like a clinical recommendation.

## How a guide gets made

1. **Start from the source.** Something gets said on an episode, in an interview,
   or on a UFC broadcast. That's the anchor. If it can't be traced to something
   public, it doesn't go in.
2. **Separate the claim from the product.** What was actually said, versus what
   the manufacturer says, versus what the research says. Those are three
   different things and they get treated as three different things.
3. **Price it honestly.** Including the cheaper option that does most of the same
   job, because most readers here are not furnishing a private studio.
4. **Say what isn't known.** If the evidence is preliminary or the mechanism is
   speculative, that goes in the guide rather than getting quietly dropped.

## Corrections

Things change. Products get discontinued, positions get updated, and sometimes a
page here is simply wrong. Every guide carries a visible "Updated" date at the
top so you can judge how stale it might be.

If something is out of date or incorrect, [tell us](/contact). Corrections get
made and the update date changes. That's the whole process.

## How this site makes money

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you buy something through one, Rogan
Recs earns a commission at no additional cost to you.

That relationship is disclosed at the top of every page that contains one, not
buried down here. It doesn't change what gets recommended, and there are plenty
of pages on this site that recommend the cheaper option or tell you to skip the
purchase entirely — which is roughly the point.
