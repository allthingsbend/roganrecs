Joe Rogan has been recommending things on a microphone for fifteen years. Some of
it stuck. Most of it is buried in a three-hour episode nobody can find again.

This site is the index. Every guide here starts from something said on the
podcast, in an interview, or on a broadcast, and works outward: what the thing
actually is, why it came up, what it costs, and whether it makes sense for a
normal person who isn't a millionaire comedian with a home gym.

## What's here

**[Supplements](/supplements)** is the biggest section and the one most people
arrive at. It covers the full stack that gets discussed, broken out by what each
compound is supposed to do, plus separate deep dives on the ones that generate
the most questions.

**[Podcast guests](/podcast-guests)** covers the recurring names — Rhonda Patrick,
Andrew Huberman, Gary Brecka — because a lot of what gets attributed to Rogan
actually came from someone sitting across the table from him. It also covers the
studio itself: the microphones, the chair, the setup.

**Recovery** gets its own guides for
[sauna](/joe-rogan-sauna-guide) and [cold plunge](/joe-rogan-cold-plunge-guide),
the two protocols that come up most and have the widest gap between what people
think they need to spend and what actually works.

**[The blog](/blog)** is everything else: hunting gear, the Comedy Mothership,
Austin restaurants, kettlebells, whiskey, elk.

## How to read anything on this site

Three things worth saying up front, because they apply to every page here.

**This is a fan site.** No connection to Joe Rogan, the JRE, or Spotify. Nobody
here has interviewed him. Everything is sourced from public episodes and
interviews, and where something is uncertain, it says so.

**Discussed is not the same as endorsed.** A guest saying something on a podcast
is not evidence that it works. Several of the protocols covered on this site have
thin or contested research behind them, and the guides say so rather than
pretending otherwise.

**It is not medical advice.** Supplements interact with medications. Cold
exposure is not safe for everyone. Peptides are a legal grey area in most places.
Talk to an actual doctor before you start anything, particularly anything you'd
be injecting.
