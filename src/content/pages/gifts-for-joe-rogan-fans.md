Most Joe Rogan gift lists have the same problem: half the products have never been anywhere near Rogan. Somebody pasted "Joe Rogan" in front of a generic mushroom tincture and called it a recommendation.

Every pick below has a documented connection to Rogan or the JRE studio, and we say how strong that connection is. Where there's money involved (Rogan has partnerships with Onnit and Kill Cliff, for example), we say that too. If the person you're buying for is a real fan, they'll know the difference.

> **The 30-second version:** Coffee drinker? [Black Rifle Just Black](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07DT75MS5). Reader? [*Empire of the Summer Moon*](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Empire+of+the+Summer+Moon+SC+Gwynne). Starting a podcast? The [Shure SM7B](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0002E4Z8M) he talks into every episode. Want something fun and cheap? A case of [Joe Rogan's Spicy Pineapple](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B093TJTMTL) Kill Cliff.

## Stocking stuffers

### Kill Cliff, Joe Rogan's Spicy Pineapple

**The Rogan connection:** His name is on the can. Kill Cliff sells this flavor as Joe Rogan's Spicy Pineapple, and older fans will know it as Flaming Joe. It's a commercial partnership, not a stray mention.

**Why it works as a gift:** It's the most "Rogan" thing on this page that costs less than a dinner out. A 12-pack disappears in a week, which is the point.

**Skip it if:** They avoid caffeine. Each can carries 150 mg, according to Kill Cliff. More in our [Kill Cliff guide](/energy-drink-joe-rogan).

[Check the 12-pack on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B093TJTMTL)

### Black Rifle Coffee, Just Black

**The Rogan connection:** In JRE #2170, Rogan identified the studio coffee as Black Rifle. Which roast he drinks isn't on record, so Just Black is our pick, not his.

**Why it works as a gift:** It gets used every single day. If you want it to feel more like a present, Black Rifle sells a [Just Black gift bundle](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DDW4N8QQ) with a tin.

**Pair it with:** [Laird Superfood turmeric creamer](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01N2B3DEG), which is the closest thing to the Laird-style cup Rogan has described making with it. Full story in our [Joe Rogan coffee guide](/joe-rogan-coffee).

[Check Just Black on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07DT75MS5)

### Neuro Gum

**The Rogan connection:** Rogan has talked up caffeinated gum as a quick hit without making another coffee, and Neuro Gum is the obvious version of that.

**Why it works as a gift:** It's caffeine plus L-theanine in a pack that fits in a gym bag or glovebox. Good for the person who's always on the road.

**Skip it if:** They're caffeine-sensitive, or they're the type to calculate cost per milligram. Coffee wins that math. See our [Neuro Gum breakdown](/supplements/neuro-gum-joe-rogan).

[Check Neuro Gum on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B071RZ2SY6)

### *Empire of the Summer Moon* by S. C. Gwynne

**The Rogan connection:** One of his longest-running book recommendations. He kept bringing it up and eventually had Gwynne on the show.

**Why it works as a gift:** It doesn't require being a JRE listener. It's a history of the Comanche and Quanah Parker that reads like a thriller.

**Also good:** *American Buffalo* by Steven Rinella as an audiobook, since Rinella narrates it himself and Rogan has said he enjoyed that version. More picks in our [Joe Rogan books guide](/joe-rogan-books).

[Find *Empire of the Summer Moon* on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Empire+of+the+Summer+Moon+SC+Gwynne)

## The solid gift

### Sennheiser HD 280 Pro headphones

**The Rogan connection:** Recent equipment inventories of the JRE studio list the HD 280 Pro as the headphones Rogan and his guests wear.

**Why it works as a gift:** They're closed-back studio headphones that make sense far beyond podcasting. Editing, practicing an instrument, or blocking out a loud house all count.

[Check the HD 280 Pro on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00IT0IHOY)

### A good kettlebell

**The Rogan connection:** Rogan has used kettlebell swings, cleans, squats and Turkish get-ups for years, and has owned Onnit's novelty Primal bells.

**Why it works as a gift:** One bell is a whole home workout. The novelty faces are fun, but a plain cast-iron bell does the same job for less. If you're unsure on weight, our [kettlebell guide](/blog/joe-rogan-kettlebells) covers how to pick.

[Browse kettlebells on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=cast+iron+kettlebell)

### The Joe Rogan Fanny Pack from Datsusara

**The Rogan connection:** Datsusara says Rogan suggested the design. It's the lower-profile version of their Joe Rogan Utility Belt.

**Why it works as a gift:** It's the rare piece of Rogan gear that's useful and not just a logo. Heads up: this one isn't on Amazon and isn't an affiliate link. We include it because it belongs on the list. Sizing and the belt vs. pack question are in our [fanny pack guide](/blog/joe-rogan-fanny-packs).

[See the fanny pack at Datsusara](https://www.dsgear.com/products/joe-rogan-fanny-pack)

### Pure Encapsulations Macular Support

**The Rogan connection:** One of the most specific supplement mentions he's made. He has said he buys it himself.

**Why it might not be a gift:** Supplements are personal. Only buy this if they've already been asking about it, and if they take other medications, let them check first. The context is in our [supplements guide](/supplements).

[Check Macular Support on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00NC2TZBS)

## The big one

### Shure SM7B (or SM7dB)

**The Rogan connection:** The SM7B is the best-documented piece of JRE gear. It's the mic in front of him and every guest.

**Which one to buy:** The SM7B needs a lot of clean gain. If they already own a decent audio interface, get the SM7B. If they don't, or you'd rather not guess, the [SM7dB](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CMFXN65Z) has a preamp built in and works with simpler setups. It isn't Rogan's mic, but it's the friendlier gift. Our [podcast equipment guide](/podcast-guests/joe-rogan-podcast-equipment) has the full setup.

[Check the SM7B on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0002E4Z8M)

### HÅG Capisco 8106

**The Rogan connection:** The saddle-style chair at the JRE desk.

**Why it works as a gift:** For someone who sits all day and keeps complaining about it. It's built for switching between sitting, perching and standing-desk height.

**Skip it if:** You're guessing. It's expensive, it looks strange, and some people hate the saddle seat. This is a "they've already sat in one" gift. Model details are in our [JRE chair guide](/podcast-guests/joe-rogan-chair-jre-podcast).

[Check the Capisco on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00OHUB0CG)

### A portable sauna

**The Rogan connection:** Rogan's routine is a traditional sauna at around 185°F. A portable unit doesn't recreate that, and we won't pretend it does.

**Why it still works as a gift:** It's the cheap way to find out whether someone will actually use heat before they spend thousands on a real one. Our [sauna guide](/joe-rogan-sauna-guide) explains what to buy after that.

[Browse portable saunas on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=portable+sauna+for+home)

## What we left off, on purpose

- **Anything sold as "Joe Rogan's favorite" supplement with no episode behind it.** That covers a lot of what turns up in search.
- **Random Rogan-branded merch from marketplace sellers.** If it isn't official, the fan you're buying for will know.
- **Whiskey.** Rogan has praised Buffalo Trace plenty (see our [whiskey guide](/blog/joe-rogan-whiskey)), but it can't ship through Amazon, so buy it locally.
- **A cold plunge tub.** It's a great gift for exactly one kind of person, and that person already knows which one they want. Our [cold plunge guide](/joe-rogan-cold-plunge-guide) covers the options.
