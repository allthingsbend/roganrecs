The **Sorinex X-Factor** got attention after Joe Rogan showed it as part of his training setup. It looks unusual because it is not a normal cable machine: the system is built around **resisted pushing, pulling and rotation**, which makes it especially relevant to punching, grappling and rotational-power training.

## What the X-Factor is designed to train

Most gym machines constrain movement to one obvious path. The X-Factor is intended to let an athlete produce force through more athletic, rotational patterns.

That makes it useful for:

- resisted punches and punch-like patterns
- rotational trunk work
- push-pull combinations
- anti-rotation training
- athletic footwork under resistance
- conditioning circuits

The key word is **specificity**. A machine like this is most compelling for someone who actually trains a sport where force is transferred through the feet, hips, trunk and upper body.

## Why Rogan likes this type of training

Rogan's training has long mixed traditional strength work with kettlebells, martial arts, mobility and unusual specialty equipment. The X-Factor fits that style because it can load movement without turning every exercise into a bodybuilding isolation drill.

For striking sports, the idea is not to make a resisted punch look exactly like a real punch. It is to build force and control through similar rotational patterns while keeping actual skill work fast and technically clean.

## Will it make you punch harder?

Not automatically.

Punching power depends on technique, timing, lower-body force, trunk rotation and sport-specific practice. Resistance training can improve strength and power qualities that support striking, but a machine cannot replace pads, bag work, sparring or coaching.

The best use is **supplemental strength and power training**.

## What to look for before buying

The X-Factor is specialty equipment, so think about the opportunity cost.

It makes the most sense if you have:

- a serious home or commercial gym
- enough permanent floor space
- a combat-sport or rotational-power training goal
- other basics already covered: rack, weights, conditioning and skill practice

It makes less sense as the first piece of equipment in a garage gym.

## Cheaper ways to train the same qualities

You can train rotation and anti-rotation for far less money with:

### Landmine setup

Landmine rotations, presses and rotational clean variations are inexpensive and easy to progress.

[Browse landmine attachments on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=barbell+landmine+attachment)

### Cable machine

A functional trainer gives you chops, lifts, presses, rows and resisted rotation while also covering dozens of general strength exercises.

### Resistance bands

Bands are excellent for anti-rotation, punch-pattern resistance and travel training, though the resistance curve feels different from a cable or specialty machine.

[Browse heavy resistance bands on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=heavy+resistance+bands+training)

### Medicine-ball throws

For actual explosive rotation, medicine-ball scoop tosses and rotational throws are simple and highly transferable when you have a safe wall or open area.

## X-Factor vs. a cable functional trainer

| | Sorinex X-Factor | Functional trainer |
|---|---|---|
| Rotational emphasis | Excellent | Very good |
| General gym versatility | Specialized | Excellent |
| Footwork freedom | Strong | Depends on cable layout |
| Cost/space efficiency | Lower | Usually better |
| Combat-sport appeal | High | Moderate to high |

For most home gyms, a functional trainer wins on versatility. The X-Factor is the more interesting choice when you already own the basics and specifically want rotational athletic work.

## Bottom line

The Sorinex X-Factor is not a magic punching-power machine. It is a **specialized rotational resistance system** that fits combat-sport and athletic training unusually well.

If you already have a complete gym and train striking or grappling, it is genuinely interesting equipment. If you are building from scratch, a rack, barbell, kettlebells, cable setup and medicine balls should come first.
