"Kill Tony" is a wildly popular live podcast and stand-up comedy show hosted by Tony Hinchcliffe, filmed at the iconic Comedy Mothership in Austin, Texas. Known for its unique format where comedians perform short sets followed by interviews, the show has captivated audiences worldwide. Securing tickets to a live recording can be a challenge due to its immense popularity, but here’s everything you need to know to improve your chances.

### **Where Is Kill Tony Filmed?**

-   **Austin, Texas**: Most "Kill Tony" episodes are filmed at the Comedy Mothership, a comedy club founded by Joe Rogan.

-   **Tour Locations**: Occasionally, "Kill Tony" travels to perform at larger venues, including NYC’s Madison Square Garden and arenas around the U.S. and the world.


### **How to Get Tickets for Kill Tony in Austin**

For shows in Austin, tickets must be purchased through the [**Comedy Mothership website**](https://comedymothership.com/). Here’s what to keep in mind:

-   **Check Early and Often**: Tickets sell out quickly, so stay vigilant on the Comedy Mothership's official site for updates.

-   **Be Prepared**: Tickets may be released without much notice, so it’s important to act fast.


### **How to Get Tickets for Kill Tony on Tour**

When "Kill Tony" hits the road, tickets can be purchased through major ticketing platforms:

-   **Ticketmaster**: Often the go-to for arena shows and special events.

-   **Other Platforms**: Websites like StubHub or Vivid Seats may also have tickets available for resale.


### **Why Are Kill Tony Tickets So Hard to Get?**

Because it’s the #1 live comedy podcast in the world!!

-   **High Demand**: With a dedicated fan base and limited seating at the Comedy Mothership, tickets are in incredibly high demand.

-   **Quick Sell-Outs**: Shows often sell out within minutes of release.

### **Tips for Securing Kill Tony Tickets**

#### **1\. Be Aware of Ticket Release Dates**

"Kill Tony" tickets sell fast, so staying informed is key.

-   **Follow Social Media**: Tony Hinchcliffe and Redban often announce ticket releases and even do countdowns on Instagram and other platforms.

-   **Join Email Lists**: Subscribe to the Comedy Mothership’s newsletter for updates on ticket sales.


#### **2\. Try the Standby Line**

If you couldn’t score a ticket, arriving early and joining the standby line is an option.

-   **What to Expect**: Sometimes tickets open up due to no-shows or last-minute cancellations, but competition in the standby line can be intense.

-   **Plan Ahead**: Bring snacks, water, and patience if you decide to try this route.
