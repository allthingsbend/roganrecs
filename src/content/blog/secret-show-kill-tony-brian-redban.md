Austin, Texas, has become a comedy mecca in recent years, and at the heart of it all is Brian Redban’s **Secret Show**, held every Thursday night at his Sunset Strip Comedy Club. Known for its unpredictable lineup and laid-back vibe, the Secret Show has become a must-see for comedy fans and a sought-after gig for up-and-coming comedians. Here’s everything you need to know about this unique event and the buzz surrounding it.

## **What is the Secret Show?**

The Secret Show is a comedy showcase curated and hosted by Brian Redban, the co-creator and producer of the **Kill Tony** podcast. Held weekly on Thursday nights, it features a mix of seasoned professionals, rising stars, and, occasionally, surprise drop-ins from some of the biggest names in comedy.

What sets the Secret Show apart is its informal, anything-can-happen atmosphere. You never know who might take the stage, and the audience gets an intimate look at comedians testing out new material or honing their craft.

## **The Kill Tony Connection**

[Fans of Kill Tony](/blog/how-to-get-kill-tony-tickets-austin-comedy-mothership-tour), the popular live podcast hosted by Tony Hinchcliffe, will recognize the Secret Show as a regular extension of the Monday night recordings. If a comedian impresses the panel during a Kill Tony taping, Redban often invites them to perform at the Secret Show.

This spur-of-the-moment booking adds a layer of excitement to Kill Tony, with many comedians vying for a chance to be noticed by Redban. However, it’s become a running joke among fans and other comedians that Redban has a tendency to invite “good-looking females” more often than not.

While there may be some truth to this playful stereotype, Redban’s invitations aren’t exclusively based on appearances. As many fans will attest, he frequently books talented male comedians and less conventionally "hot" female performers who deliver standout sets.

## **Redban’s Personal Life: Setting the Record Straight**

The “good-looking female” joke persists, but it’s worth noting that Brian Redban is happily married to his wife, Janice. Despite the playful ribbing he gets on Kill Tony and beyond, Redban’s true focus for the Secret Show is showcasing comedic talent, regardless of gender or appearance.

## **The Sunset Strip Comedy Club Experience**

Located in Austin, the **Sunset Strip Comedy Club** serves as the venue for the Secret Show. It’s a space that reflects Redban’s unfiltered, anything-goes style. However, the club isn’t without its quirks, and it’s become the subject of jokes among comedians.

One of the most notable critics is **Yannis Pappas**, a stand-up comedy powerhouse who loves to poke fun at the venue. Pappas has joked about the **high ceilings** and **background noise**, playfully suggesting that they distract from the performances. But for fans and comedians who embrace the raw, unpolished nature of live comedy, these quirks are part of the charm.

## **Why You Should Check Out the Secret Show**

If you’re in Austin on a Thursday night, the Secret Show offers a comedy experience unlike any other. Here’s why it’s worth your time:

-   **Unpredictable Lineups**: You could see rising stars, Kill Tony favorites, or even surprise appearances by big-name comedians.

-   **Comedy in its Rawest Form**: The relaxed atmosphere encourages comedians to experiment and try out new material.

-   **Community Vibes**: The Sunset Strip Comedy Club is a hub for Austin’s thriving comedy scene, and the Secret Show is at its center.


Brian Redban’s Secret Show is more than just another comedy night—it’s a celebration of the art of stand-up and a cornerstone of Austin’s comedy renaissance. Whether you’re a fan of Kill Tony, a lover of live comedy, or simply curious about what makes Austin’s comedy scene so special, the Secret Show is not to be missed.

And if you’re lucky, you might just catch one of Redban’s Kill Tony picks—good-looking female or not—bringing their A-game to the stage.
